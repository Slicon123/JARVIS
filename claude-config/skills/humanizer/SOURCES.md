# Sources

Not needed at runtime. Why the skill is built the way it is.

- [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) v3.0.0, MIT, by Siqi Chen: §1 to §25, the workflow, and "When not to act". License text in `LICENSE-blader-humanizer`.
- [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), maintained by [WikiProject AI Cleanup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:WikiProject_AI_Cleanup): the evidence behind §1 to §25.
- Russell, Karpinska and Iyyer, ["People who frequently use ChatGPT for writing tasks are accurate and robust detectors of AI-generated text"](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.267/) (ACL 2025): five experienced readers, voting together, misjudged 1 of 300 articles from GPT-4o, Claude and o1. They used AI vocabulary and also formality, originality, and clarity. That is why this skill works on structure and not only on word lists.
- [Pangram: Can AI detection catch Claude writing styles?](https://www.pangram.com/blog/claude-writing-styles): Claude's built-in and custom styles were still detected, which is the reason for the detector warning at the top.
- Liang, Yuksekgonul, Mao, Wu and Zou, "GPT detectors are biased against non-native English writers", *Patterns* 4(7), 2023 ([summary](https://techxplore.com/news/2023-07-gpt-detectors-biased-non-native-english.pdf)): seven detectors flagged plain-vocabulary human essays as AI; asking for richer word choice turned about 88% of them "human". Basis for detector.md steps 2 and 3.
- Turnitin language support: [Arabic launch, Aug 2026](https://www.turnitin.com.au/press/turnitin-expands-ai-writing-detection-to-support-arabic-language-submissions) lists English, Spanish, Japanese and Arabic; [community post](https://turnitin.forumbee.com/t/83yjxa1/its-live-ai-writing-detection-for-arabic-submissions): papers in unsupported languages are not processed for AI writing.
- Measured 23 Oct 2026 on turnitindetector.ai (T-Checker): Bryan's PMB omnichannel essay (Indonesian, ~1,050 words) scored 46/50/4 (human/mixed/AI) before a tells-only humanize pass and 41/55/4 after. The pass also created a new repeated opening ("Di pemasaran, ... Di keuangan, ... Di SDM, ..."), which is why step 3 now lists paragraph openings.
- §26 to §36 are JARVIS additions from observation, 25 Sep 2026 (the original §26, praise wrappers, was folded into §22). Not measured; *weak alone* unless marked.
