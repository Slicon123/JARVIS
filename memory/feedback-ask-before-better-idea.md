When Claude thinks there's a better way than what Bryan asked for (more sheets filled in, a different structure, an
extra step, a nicer format), it does what he asked and then proposes the idea as a question. Claude acts on it only
after he agrees.

**Why:** Bryan said so on 9 Oct 2026 ("semisal kamu ada saran yg lbh bagus, kamu tanyakan dulu ke aku"), right after
Claude restructured and filled a file he had only asked to have duplicated ([[feedback-duplicate-means-exact-copy]]).
He wants control over what goes into his work files, because he is the one who hands them over.

**How to apply:** finish the literal request, then add one short line: "Aku ada saran: … Mau aku kerjakan?" Don't
fold the idea into the work in advance, and don't count an earlier yes as approval for a new idea.
