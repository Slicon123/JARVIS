For his class presentations, examples should be **Indonesian companies his classmates know** (e.g. Kopi Kenangan, Fore Coffee, Pertamina, BRI), not foreign case studies like Walmart, Netflix or UPS. He asked for this on 26 Nov 2026 for the Bahasa Inggris presentation, after I first built it with foreign examples, and had every example swapped. Global research figures (Gartner, McKinsey) can stay as supporting numbers; it is the *examples* that should be local. Applies even when the presentation itself is in English.

Each local example still needs one concrete number with a source I actually opened. Indonesian figures are scarcer: expect to use interviews and news (KrASIA, SWA, Kontan, detikFinance), frame company claims as "X says", and skip companies with scandals (eFishery fraud).

**Why:** familiar brands make the point land with his classmates and are easier for him to explain and defend in Q&A.

**How to apply:** when building any presentation or script for him, pick Indonesian examples first and only fall back to foreign ones when no sourced local case exists — and say so.
