When Bryan asks for a new file that is a "duplikat" of an example so he can edit it, make a byte-for-byte copy
and stop there. Don't fill in data, delete or rename sheets, or add anything, even when you already know what
should go in it.

**Why:** On 9 Oct 2026 he asked for guidance only ("kamu tdk perlu mengubah apapun, hanya memberitahu aku"),
then for a duplicate of the FSS approval form he could edit. Claude cut it from 23 to 4 sheets, renamed the tabs
and filled the genset data. He pushed back: "jangan mengubah bentukkan file excel yg baru dari contohnya, jgn
menambahkan juga". He wants to do the input himself, guided, so he learns it and controls what goes out.

**How to apply:** If he wants guidance, give guidance; if he wants a copy, give a copy. Offer the fill-in as a
separate step and wait for a yes ([[feedback-ask-before-better-idea]]). Related: [[project-approval-material-forms]].
