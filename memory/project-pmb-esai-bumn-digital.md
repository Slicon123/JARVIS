On 23 Oct 2026 Bryan asked for a plain-text answer (to paste into a Google Form) to a PMB
question: government assigns telecom/bank BUMN to lead a national data-center and digital
payment consortium; private tech associations call it hidden monopoly. Evaluate BUMN's ideal
role per Ismail Solihin, distortion vs agent of development, and a governance solution
(PPP/KPBU or infrastructure-vs-service split). Not yet confirmed submitted.

Line taken: BUMN is a catalyst at the infrastructure layer and in unserved areas (UU 19/2003
Pasal 2 "perintis ... yang belum dapat dilaksanakan oleh sektor swasta"), a distortion risk in
commercial services. Solutions: open-access infrastructure vs competed services (QRIS as the
model), structural separation of BUMN subsidiaries, KPBU with availability payment (Palapa Ring),
transparent PSO (Pasal 66), least-subsidy auction for 3T, regulator/owner/operator split,
KPPU review, Pasal 51 UU 5/1999 exclusivity only by statute, 3–5 year review.

Facts checked 23 Oct 2026: UU 16/2025 (4th amendment, 6 Oct 2025) turned Kementerian BUMN into
Badan Pengaturan BUMN; 1% Dwiwarna via BP BUMN, 99% seri B managed by Danantara. Perpres
38/2015 (KPBU) still listed in force. Solihin (2014) *Pengantar Bisnis* is Erlangga (library
catalogs, ISBN 978-602-298-285-2) — same entry as in [[project-pmb-tugas-badan-usaha]].
The "perintis" aim is the UU's wording, not verified as Solihin's own text.
He then asked to humanize it; the humanized version dropped the Penutup paragraph.

**Second GForm question, same day:** CSR of a multinational cloud data center in a village
(groundwater drawdown, local heat, sembako charity). Cited Solihin, I. (2009) *Corporate Social
Responsibility from Charity to Sustainability*, Salemba Empat (ISBN 9789790610385; some catalogs
say 2011). Also Carroll 1991, Elkington 1997, Porter & Kramer 2006/2011, UU 40/2007 Pasal 74,
UU 25/2007 Pasal 15, UU 17/2019 (business water use must not override the people's right to
water). Written straight in humanized style; he still asked to humanize it. Expect more questions from this GForm.

**Third question, same day:** "Nusantara Mart" retailer going omnichannel (Solihin's business
definition, scope across operasional/pemasaran/keuangan/SDM, supply-chain risks, mitigation).
Solihin's exact definition of bisnis could not be found online: the "create value ... melalui
transaksi" wording is Amirullah & Hardjanto (2005), and "kegiatan usaha individu yang
terorganisasi..." is Hughes & Kapoor. So the essay paraphrases per the dosen's indicator, with
no quote marks. Used Verhoef, Kannan & Inman (2015) for multichannel vs omnichannel, UU 27/2022 PDP.
