Built 15 Nov 2026: `animate/tools/openverse.py` fetches CC BY / CC0 / public-domain instrumental tracks from Openverse (mostly Jamendo). Only the tool is tested. Whether YouTube raises Content ID claims on these tracks is **unknown** (status 15 Nov 2026: "Drakkar" by Ivan Tregub, 10 s clip, uploaded private to @rupanya.gini, passed YouTube's Initial check with Copyright: No issues found; claims can still appear later, so recheck the Restrictions column of the private video "tes openverse drakkar" on 16 Nov and keep that video until then; other tracks are untested).

**Why:** Bryan makes films in parallel: `riset-sekitar` gives 5 ideas, 3 go into 3 separate chats at once, the other 2 later. Chats cannot see each other, so if Openverse music goes into all of them before a result exists, one claim hits every film.

**How to apply:** `sekitar` films never use it (they synthesise their own bed, no licence needed). Use Openverse music only when Bryan asks. Each track gets its own 3-minute check first: a short clip with that track, uploaded Private to @rupanya.gini, then read the Initial check step in Studio. Until the 16 Nov recheck is clean, use it on ONE film at a time, never across a parallel batch. Update this file with each track's result. See [[project-konten-edukasi-sekitar]] and [[project-animate-skill]].
