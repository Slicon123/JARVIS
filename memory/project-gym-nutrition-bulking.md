Body stats as of 4 September 2026: **54 kg, 172 cm** — BMI 18.3, just under the 18.5
underweight line. Age assumed 18 for the calculation; never confirmed by him.

**Targets: ~2,800 kcal/day and ~90 g protein/day.** Maintenance worked out at ~2,370 kcal
(Mifflin-St Jeor BMR 1,530 × 1.55 activity, for the 4-day split in
[[project-gym-hypertrophy]]). Goal rate +0.3 kg/week, weighed Sunday mornings.

**Constraint: he has already paid for a catering plan, so he is reluctant to spend on extra
food on top of it.** Two complete meals per day arrive by catering (nasi, lauk, sayur). He
corrected an earlier note that said he *cannot* cook on 4 September 2026 — he can; he simply
won't, because the catering already covers his meals and buying twice over is wasteful. So
the constraint is budget and duplication, not a missing kitchen. Check whether the catering
service sells a 3-meals-per-day package before proposing anything he'd have to buy
separately.

Final plan as of 4 September 2026, with his own status on each item:

- **Three meals a day instead of two. Committed.** The third is ayam crispy + nasi, double
  portion — chosen over Indomie + telur, which carries 12 g protein against the chicken's
  25 g. Three meals puts him at roughly 2,100 kcal, leaving a ~700 kcal gap.
- **3 telur + a handful of kacang tanah daily. Committed.** ~800 kcal and 18 g protein for
  around Rp 12,000/day, which closes the gap. Chosen specifically because they are the
  cheapest protein and the cheapest no-cook calories available to him.
- **Sunday morning weigh-in. Committed**, and he was asked to start before the creatine
  arrives so the 1–2 kg water jump doesn't contaminate his baseline.
- Susu full cream — **deferred; he'll say when.** If he revisits it, susu bubuk full cream in
  a large sachet is far cheaper per glass than UHT cartons.
- Creatine monohydrate 5 g/day — **ordered but not delivered; he'll say when it arrives.**
- Ruled out on cost: roti tawar + selai kacang (peanuts are the same food, cheaper), and
  UHT milk cartons.

**Why:** Eating twice a day left him roughly 900 kcal/day short of growth. That gap, not a
missing supplement, is why he is skinny — he came in asking about creatine on a friend's
recommendation. Creatine is worth about +1.4 kg lean mass over months (pooled across 61
trials); the food is worth several times that.

**He is a student on a tight budget** — his own words, "im just a normal mahasiswa." Cost is
the binding constraint on every food suggestion, so price anything new in rupiah per calorie
before recommending it, and say plainly that the figures are rough and worth checking locally.

**How to apply:** Ask for the Sunday weight once a couple of weeks have passed. If it is
flat, add food — do not change the training. Don't nag about the deferred items; he said he
would update, and he was told the objection once already. Keep the explanations plain, per
[[feedback-plain-language-explanations]].
