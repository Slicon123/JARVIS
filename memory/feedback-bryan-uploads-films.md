Every sekitar film for @rupanya.gini is uploaded by Bryan himself (his rule, 17 Nov 2026: "semua film aku yang upload"). A film session stops once the mp4, cover and `upload.md` are done and pass their checks, then reports their paths. It does not run `unggah-sekitar`, does not open YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or Meta Business Suite to post or schedule, and does not call `pipeline_update` with stage `upload`.

**Why:** he wants control over what goes out on his account and when. It came up on 17 Nov 2026 while 5 film sessions were running in parallel.

**How to apply:** this holds for all films, including ones started from riset-sekitar briefs. Write the rule into every film brief you hand to a new session. Only his own explicit request in chat for one specific upload overrides it, and only for that upload.
