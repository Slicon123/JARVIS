In `upload.md`, a film with no Facebook product has a single `## Instagram & Facebook` section. The reel goes up once with Share to Facebook on, so there is no separate `## Facebook` block. Only a product film keeps `## Instagram` and `## Facebook` apart, because only the Facebook text carries the keranjang line and Shopee link.

**Why:** on 20 Nov 2026 the spidol-papan and mata-kucing-jalan films had their own Facebook captions, almost identical to Instagram's. Bryan mixed them up with the product films and thought the Shopee link was missing. He asked for one combined caption on every film without a product.

**How to apply:** this is written into `sekitar`'s upload-text rules, its keran example and `upload_check.py`, which still finds the combined heading. The rule was applied the same day to spidol-papan, mata-kucing-jalan and parabola-hujan. See [[feedback-make-affiliate-link]], [[feedback-paste-upload-text]].
