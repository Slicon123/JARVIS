For every @rupanya.gini film that carries a Facebook product, I make the Shopee affiliate link myself before delivering: Claude in Chrome > affiliate.shopee.co.id/offer/product_offer (Penawaran Produk) > search > pick the item whose bottle/shape matches the 3D object in the film > Buat Link (Pakai Tag: Tidak) > read the `s.shopee.co.id` short link from the textbox. Put it on the Facebook keranjang line of upload.md, and name the product (title, price, sold, commission) in Pengaturan. Never ship `<link afiliasi Shopee>` as a placeholder.

**Why:** on 20 Nov 2026 the lem-korea film was delivered with a placeholder; Bryan: "biasanya juga kamu kasih ke aku, kok skrg tbtb engga? jangan diulangi lagi lain kali". He was already logged in, and making a link takes about a minute.

**How to apply:** do it during step 7 (Deliver) of `sekitar`, before pasting upload.md. Pick the product that matches the film's object over a slightly higher commission. Skip oil-based "lem korea" style traps: check the item is really the thing the film explains. If Shopee shows an anti-bot check or a login page, ask Bryan to clear it, then continue. See [[project-facebook-affiliate-shopee]] and [[feedback-paste-upload-text]].
