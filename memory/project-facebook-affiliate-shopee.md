**How to tag a product (verified on his screen)**
- Only at creation: Facebook > Buat reel > upload > screen Pengaturan > **Tambahkan produk** (above Bagikan ke cerita). **Jadwalkan** is on the same screen, so affiliate Reels can be scheduled from Facebook itself.
- A published Reels' three-dot menu has no product option. `Edit postingan` (caption) not tried. Don't re-upload a film just for a tag (duplicate risk).
- Fallback for an old film: make a link in affiliate.shopee.co.id (Penawaran Produk > Buat Link) and pin it as the first comment. Whether Facebook limits link comments and whether commission counts is unverified.
