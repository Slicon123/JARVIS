On 1 Nov 2026 Bryan said the blur on camera transitions in his `sekitar` films makes him a bit dizzy, and asked for `minyak-terbakar` without it. `out/minyak-terbakar/minyak-terbakar_v2.mp4` has `Frame smear={false}`; the older `minyak-terbakar.mp4` keeps the blur. He has not said whether this applies to every film, or reported watching `_v2`.

**Why:** the blur also hid how fast the camera moved. Peak speed on that film was 35.5 screen-widths per second, so turning blur off alone would likely have been worse.

**How to apply:** when using `smear={false}`, also slow the moves and shorten long travel. That film went from 35.5 to 7.6 widths per second by moving the spoon scene closer (40 to 16 units) and lengthening moves to about 1–1.6 s. Ask him before making it the default for all films. See [[project-konten-edukasi-sekitar]].
