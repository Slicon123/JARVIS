On 10 Oct 2026 Bryan was asked at work to turn a 12-page genset IOM PDF ("Sistem Kerja dan Cara Pengoperasian
Genset": DSE 7320 controller, Perkins 4006-23TAG2A engine, Stamford alternator) into Word, branded PT. Sinergi
Sarana Solusi. The changes were the company name, a PERKINS logo top-left and the SSS logo top-right on every page,
and the header/footer name. Result: `Laporan Sinergi\SOP\IOM - SSS 7320 - SP750P.docx`. The flowcharts are
editable shapes, the troubleshooting pages are Word tables, and the DSE photo with its numbered arrows is one picture.

Bryan's choices: the previous company's slogan ("GENERATOR SET SPECIALIST / We Believe In Quality") stays on the
cover. The p.3 *KETERANGAN legend was renumbered to match the photo (a stray "Ready." line had shifted items 4–9).
About 24 obvious typos were fixed. The p.5 overlap "AUTO Pada.PKG" became "AUTO Pada PKG dan Genset.". Technical
data was left untouched.

Logos: PERKINS is Wikimedia Commons `Perkins-Logo.svg` (public domain). SSS is the 199×189 px logo from
`Laporan PM Grounding Dan Penyalur Petir.docx`, upscaled and cleaned. That was the largest copy in his files.

**Why:** follow-up edits are likely. The build scripts lived in a session scratchpad and are gone.

**How to apply:** for small edits, edit the Word file directly. For a new conversion like this, follow
[[feedback-pdf-to-word-replica]]. Related: [[project-fss-ats-documentation]], [[feedback-ask-before-better-idea]].
