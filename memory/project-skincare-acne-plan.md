Started 20 September 2026 from three selfies he sent. He asked for a dermatologist-level,
evidence-backed answer specific to him, not generic skincare advice.

**Assessment from the photos:** mild inflammatory acne — small papules and pustules on the
chin, the perioral area (around the mouth), and scattered on both cheeks. **No nodules, no
cysts, no atrophic scarring** visible, which puts this firmly in the mild band. Separately,
**post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH)** — the flat brown marks, heaviest on the right
cheek, plus older marks at the left temple and hairline. Skin tone roughly Fitzpatrick IV,
so PIH is the slow part: 3–12 months to clear even with perfect care. Oily T-zone.

**The key finding, and the reason the plan is shaped the way it is:** he reported oily
*and* tight/dry/flaky *and* stinging with new products, while using facial wash with
nothing after it. That triad is a **stripped skin barrier**, not sensitive skin. It matters
because irritation drives melanin production in his skin tone — so aggressive actives would
make the dark marks he cares about *worse*. The barrier gets repaired before anything
active goes on. Do not let him skip Phase 0 or ramp faster than the schedule.

**Constraints he set:** OTC only, cheapest viable option, no dermatologist visit. Salatiga,
lives in a kost. Products priced in rupiah and checked for Indonesian availability.

Phase schedule as given:

- **Weeks 1–2 (from 20 Sep 2026) — repair only, no acne treatment.** Hada Labo Gokujyun
  face wash (~Rp 30k), Wardah Cica Complex + Panthenol Ceramide moisturizer (~Rp 30–50k),
  Azarine Hydrasoothe Sunscreen Gel SPF 45 (~Rp 35–75k). Morning = water rinse only. Max
  twice-daily washing, no scrubs, nothing that tingles.
- **From ~4 Oct 2026 — benzoyl peroxide.** Benzolac 2.5% (~Rp 23–31k), *short contact*:
  5 minutes then rinse, every other night, building to leave-on by week 5. Bleaches fabric.
- **From ~1 Nov 2026 — adapalene**, only if his skin has been calm for 2 straight weeks.
  Evalen gel 0.1% (~Rp 61k; classified obat keras but sold at most apotek). Pea-size for the
  whole face, sandwiched between moisturizer, 2 nights/week ramping to nightly over 8 weeks.
  Warn again about the week 2–6 purge — that is when people quit.
- **Judge the whole thing 13 December 2026**, not before. Photos every two weeks, same spot
  and same light, because he will not see gradual change in a mirror.

**His habits, answered 20 September 2026:**

- **He does pick** — his words, when a pimple feels like it wants to come out he'll "tarik
  jerawat itu keluar." He does not think of this as squeezing. It is, and it is the main
  source of his dark marks. Countered with hydrocolloid patches as a replacement behaviour
  rather than a bare "don't."
- **No hair product** beyond Tresemme shampoo and conditioner — but conditioner runoff is a
  live suspect for the temple and hairline marks, since conditioner is designed to leave a
  film behind. Free fix: wash hair first, rinse with head tilted back, wash face last.
  Verdict due around mid-November 2026.
- **Never shaves** — so the bumps around his mouth and chin are true acne, not ingrown
  hairs. That differential is ruled out; don't revisit it.
- **Not drinking milk at the moment** (it ran out), so the dairy experiment is running by
  accident. Ask *when* it ran out — 6+ weeks with no change means dairy is not his driver
  and he should never bother restricting it.

**Why:** the diet evidence he'll find online is weaker than it looks — a 2025 meta-analysis
found no significant pooled association for glycemic load, dairy, or fatty acids. He is
54 kg chasing 2,800 kcal in [[project-gym-nutrition-bulking]], so the bulk outranks a
contested acne theory. Never let a skin suggestion cut his calories.

**How to apply:** don't add products — his skin punishes stacking, and one new thing at a
time, two weeks apart, is the rule. Escalate to a real dokter kulit only if 12 consistent
weeks produce nothing, or if nodules, pits, or true scarring appear. Keep the explanations
plain per [[feedback-plain-language-explanations]]. He approved putting this in the public
repo when the privacy tradeoff was put to him.
