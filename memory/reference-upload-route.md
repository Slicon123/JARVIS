Learned while scheduling kartu, helm-jatuh and bensin-lama on 3 Nov 2026 (see [[project-konten-edukasi-sekitar]]). **Since 3 Nov 2026 the working procedure is the `unggah-sekitar` skill** (built at his request after the jentik-bak upload; local, not pushed); these notes are the history behind it. Its `prep.py` cuts the film into ≤9 MB parts (3 calls per platform instead of 6), and the SHA-256 is compared inside the page, because the Chrome tool blanks long hex strings it returns.

- **Files over 10 MB:** Chrome's `file_upload` refuses more than 10 MB per call, and a browser_batch counts all its uploads together. Split the mp4 with `split -n <k> -d` into ~5 MB parts in the scratchpad, add a hidden `<input type=file id=claude-stage>` to the page that stores each file in `window.__chunks`, upload one part per call, rebuild with `new File([...parts])`, compare its SHA-256 with `sha256sum` of the original, then set it on the site's own file input through `DataTransfer` and dispatch `change`. Worked on YouTube Studio, TikTok Studio and instagram.com. Staged parts are lost when the page navigates.
- **YouTube:** `youtube.com/upload` can open a channel that is not his content channel. Check the avatar is the orange "r." / Rupanya Gini before uploading, or go straight to `studio.youtube.com/channel/UCwBcNfWiZ7y3tolo-hnanuw/videos/upload?d=ud`. Custom thumbnails need the channel's phone verification (his to do; not done as of 3 Nov 2026); "Select from video" offers only 3 automatic frames. Schedule time is a 15-minute list; pick "(GMT+0700) Local Time". Type title/description with the type action, then compare SHA-256 of `#textbox` text with the upload.md block.
- **TikTok Studio:** the Draft.js caption reads back correctly as the `[data-block=true]` texts joined by `\n` (innerText adds extra blank lines). Cover: Edit cover → image input → Save (the Save button sits above the viewport in a short window; click it with JS). AI-generated content sits under "Show more". **Scheduled posts can't be edited**, so settle everything before pressing Schedule.
- **Instagram:** instagram.com has no schedule option and no drafts. **Meta Business Suite is now the scheduling route:** he logged in on 3 Nov 2026 (portfolio Rupanya Gini, linked to IG rupanya.gini and the Facebook Page). Tested the same day with jentik-bak up to the Schedule picker and discarded at his word ("tes aja, jangan upload beneran"); Scheduled and Drafts were empty afterwards. Its Add Video makes a file input only on click, so `stage.js` intercepts the click to avoid the native picker. Caption and cover there are not tested yet. Steps are in the `unggah-sekitar` skill.
- **Posting live (jentik-bak, 3 Nov 2026):** Chrome's YouTube may be signed in to his personal Google account; Studio then says "you don't have permission". Fix: youtube.com avatar → Switch account → Rupanya Gini (under the backup Google account). TikTok "Post" asks "Continue to post?" while Content check lite runs; after posting it shows "Only me / Content under review" for a few minutes, then Everyone. instagram.com Create → Post opens a crop that is not 9:16: pick Original. The caption counter under the box (e.g. 808/2,200) is the reliable length check; the DOM drops blank lines. Web has no Share-to-feed toggle; Share to Facebook Page defaults on. First comments: YouTube watch page, IG reel page, TikTok video page (open the comments panel, then click the box itself before typing).
- **Phone (Samsung A36, shown as "Bryan's A36"):** PowerShell `Shell.Application` → Namespace(17) → "Bryan's A36" → Internal storage → Movies → `CopyHere`, then check `ExtendedProperty("System.Size")` against the original bytes.
