Bahasa Inggris (DB105C), 2 SKS, dosen Brigitta Putri Atika Tyagita — Rabu 13–15 at FTI424. English for digital business: reading → grammar → professional speaking/presentation. Syllabus captured 2 September 2026 from the course PDF (DB105_20261.pdf).

**Meeting topics:** 1 intro & course contract · 2 reading strategies + comprehension (main idea, specific info, vocabulary, inference, reference, author's purpose, conclusion) · 3 academic reading; skimming, scanning, structure, tense · 4 **Reading Test** + vocabulary (word families, context clues) · 5 Simple Present + **Grammar Quiz** · 6 Simple Past + **Grammar Quiz** · 7 **Test 1 (TTS)** · 8 functional expressions: introduction, request, opinions, agree/disagree (DB topic) · 9 professional communication: clarifications, request, opinions · 10 pronunciation, fluency, conversation strategies · 11 presentation language: opening, signposting, transition, closing · 12 presentation organization, audience awareness, visual support · 13 DB topic/product script & delivery (**in-class writing**) · 14 presentation rehearsal (self-study, **class OFF**) · 15 **formal presentation — DB topic/product (Project)** · 16 **Test 2 (TAS)**.

**Meeting-number anchor:** the dosen stated that **Wednesday 2 September 2026 = meeting 2**. So meeting N falls on 2 Sep + (N-2) weeks, assuming no holiday/exam-week skips: m4 Reading Test 16 Sep · m5 23 Sep · m6 30 Sep · m7 Test 1/TTS 7 Oct · m13 in-class writing 18 Nov · m14 class OFF 25 Nov · m15 presentation project 2 Des · m16 Test 2/TAS 9 Des. Re-anchor if a week gets skipped.

**Assessment:** Reading Test 5%, Grammar Quiz 1 10%, Grammar Quiz 2 10%, Writing 10%, Project 15%, TTS 25%, TAS 25%. Underlying CPMK weights: DB1051 20% (reading + project), DB1052 30% (grammar + writing), DB1053 50% (TTS + TAS).

**Grade scale (different from other courses — has a CD, and C floors at 60):** A 80–100, AB 75–79.9, B 70–74.9, BC 65–69.9, C 60–64.9, CD 55–59.9, D 50–54.9, E 0–49.9.

**Rules that bite:** attendance is self-taken on **flearn** per the siasat schedule and is weighted — Present 100%, Late 50%, Excused 25%, Absent 0%; late = 10+ minutes after start. **No make-up quiz** except with a doctor's certificate. Projects/assignments graded by rubric — ask for it before submitting. Keep all submission and assessment documentation, and calculate your own mark before disputing a grade.

**Why:** So questions about this class's schedule, weighting, or "what do I need on the TAS" can be answered without re-uploading slides.

**How to apply:** Match the week to the meeting number. Remember meeting 14 has no class and meeting 15 is the graded presentation. Class time is in [[project-uksw-digital-business]]; the sibling course syllabus is [[project-matematika-logika-db103a]].
